# Brianna Blackwood-Mallory

**Fractional Head of Talent | Recruiting Operations & DEI Strategy**

Brianna Blackwood-Mallory is an Atlanta-based fractional Head of Talent and recruiting and DEI strategist, and the founder of Königin Consulting & Recruiting. She has recruited for Google, Microsoft, and Input Output Global (IOHK), and builds fair, measurable hiring systems for startup and growth-stage companies. She writes the LinkedIn newsletters The Kaleidoscope Effect and Proof of Work.

### Focus areas
Recruiting operations · DEI strategy and inclusive hiring · AI in hiring · Technical and go-to-market recruiting · Employer brand
